Bass Lake is a 187-acre seepage lake in Washburn County, Wisconsin.

| Fact | Details |
|---|---|
| Area | 187 acres |
| Maximum depth | 35 feet |
| Lake type | Seepage |
| Bottom | 30% sand, 30% gravel, 40% rock, 0% muck |
| Fish | Musky, panfish, largemouth bass, and walleye |
| Boat landings | 1 |
| Invasive species | Banded mystery snail and Chinese mystery snail |

Check the posted signs at the boat landing for local ordinances.
